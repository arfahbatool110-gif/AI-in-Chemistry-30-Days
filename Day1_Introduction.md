## 🧠 What I Learned Today
1. **Cheminformatics:** It is the bridge between Chemistry and Computer Science. It allows us to convert chemical structures, molecular formulas, and properties into a format that computers can understand.
2. **AI in Drug Discovery:** Traditional drug discovery takes 10-15 years and billions of dollars. AI can scan millions of chemical structures in minutes to predict which molecule can fight a disease.
3. **SMILES Strings:** Computers don't see rings or bonds; they read text. For example, Paracetamol is represented as a text string: `CC(=O)NC1=CC=C(O)C=C1`.
